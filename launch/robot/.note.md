### RobotModel _(Static Structure)_

- **Source:** Loaded once from your URDF (`robot_description`) and SRDF
  (`robot_description_semantic`) files.
- **Function:** Defines the kinematic tree of the robot—including joint limits,
  parent-child link relationships, and joint types.
- **Mutability:** **Immutable.** It never changes while your node is running.

---

### RobotState _(Dynamic Snapshot)_

- **Source:** Instantiated dynamically using the configuration defined by
  `RobotModel`.
- **Function:** Represents the robot at a single point in time, storing joint
  positions, velocities, accelerations, and transformations.
- **Mutability:** **Mutable.** Used to evaluate "what-if" scenarios (e.g., _"If
  Joint 1 is at $45^\circ$, where is the end-effector?"_).
